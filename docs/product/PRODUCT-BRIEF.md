# Product brief

## Product

Davetim is a mobile-first tool for creating digital invitations, sharing them through a link, and tracking guest responses.

## Primary audience

People in Turkey organizing weddings, engagements, birthdays, graduations, baby events, and similar occasions who want a polished invitation without learning professional design software.

## Core job

“Help me publish a beautiful invitation quickly and keep guest responses organized.”

## Product principles

1. The creation and RSVP path remains useful without payment or mandatory advertising.
2. Users see an ad only after an explicit choice and know the exact reward before opting in.
3. The mobile product is native and task-focused, not a wrapper around the retired website.
4. Existing user data remains intact throughout migration.
5. Store claims, screenshots, and download links describe only shipped functionality.

## Current status

- The retired web frontend is archived and its automated deployment is disabled.
- The mobile Expo foundation contains navigation, initial design tokens, a Supabase client boundary, and a disabled rewarded-ad gateway.
- The new landing page is implemented; store buttons remain intentionally inactive until real listings exist.
- No production database migration and no live advertising integration has been applied.
